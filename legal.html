00 — Nexa Player Privacy (Zero Data Collection)

NEXA

Nexa Player plays IPTV playlists that you add from your own provider. Nexa does not provide, host, or sell any content.

We collect nothing. Nexa has no account system, no analytics, no crash or error reporting, no advertising, and no tracking. It does not send any information to us, and we have no server that it talks to.

What Nexa stores on your device:

  • Your playlists: Each playlist's name, type, and server address. Usernames, passwords, and playlist addresses that can contain credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain (on Android, in storage encrypted with the Android Keystore). They are kept on this device only: they are not synced through iCloud Keychain and do not move to a new device.
  • Your activity: Favourites, recently watched channels, Continue Watching, the position where you stopped each film or episode, and your settings.
  • Temporary copies: Your playlist's channel and film list, TV-guide listings, and channel logos and posters, kept so Nexa opens quickly. Your device may clear them at any time. The channel list and TV guide are not included in backups.

Who Nexa connects to: Only the IPTV provider whose playlist you add. That means its playlist or API, its streams, its TV guide, and the image addresses it lists for logos and posters. These requests go directly from your device to your provider under its own privacy policy. We never receive them. Opening the Privacy Policy or Terms of Service from inside Nexa loads this page in your browser.

Deleting your data: Deleting a playlist in Nexa removes its saved details, its credentials, its favourites, history, resume points, and offline list. Deleting the app removes everything else. If credentials remain in the Keychain after you delete the app, Nexa removes them the next time it is installed and opened.

Children: Nexa is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

04 — AI Features

AI

Some premium features are powered by AI (the Task Atomizer, automatic session tagging, and coaching insights).

  • When you use these features, the text you enter (such as task titles) and your anonymous focus-session history are sent to our third-party AI provider (DeepSeek) — through our own secure backend — solely to generate the result you requested.
  • This text is processed only to provide the feature. It is not used to identify you, is not linked to your identity, and is never sold.
  • AI requests are routed through our backend, so your device never stores or shares an AI provider key.
