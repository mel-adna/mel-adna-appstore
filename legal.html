01 — What We Collect

DATA

We believe in data minimization. We only collect what is absolutely necessary to make the app function.

  • Usage Data: Anonymous analytics to help us identify crashes and improve performance properly.
  • Device Info: Model, OS version, and locale to ensure compatibility.

02 — What We Never Collect

PRIVACY

Your private life is yours. We strictly do not collect:

  • Personal Usage History (browser history, other apps).
  • Location Data (GPS coordinates).
  • Contacts or Photos (unless you explicitly select a photo to edit).
  • Biometric Data (FaceID/TouchID data remains on your device).

03 — Third Parties

SECURITY

We do not sell your data. Never have, never will.

We may use trusted third-party services for:

  • Apple iCloud: To sync your data across your devices.
  • RevenueCat: To process subscription status (managed anonymously).

04 — AI Features

AI

Some premium features are powered by AI (the Task Atomizer, automatic session tagging, and coaching insights).

  • When you use these features, the text you enter (such as task titles) and your anonymous focus-session history are sent to our third-party AI provider (DeepSeek) — through our own secure backend — solely to generate the result you requested.
  • This text is processed only to provide the feature. It is not used to identify you, is not linked to your identity, and is never sold.
  • AI requests are routed through our backend, so your device never stores or shares an AI provider key.
