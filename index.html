FAQ

// quick answers
How long until I get a reply?
+
We aim to respond within 24 hours on business days. Add "URGENT" to your subject line if it's time-sensitive.
How do I request a refund?
+
App Store purchases are managed by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to submit a refund request. You can also email us and we'll guide you through it.
How do I report a bug?
+
Email us your device model, iOS version, and the steps that caused the bug. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot and speeds up the fix.
How do I cancel my subscription?
+
Go to Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions on your iPhone, find the app, and tap Cancel Subscription. Refunds are handled directly through Apple.
Do you collect personal data?
+
Only the minimum needed to run the app. Read our Privacy Policy for a full breakdown. We never sell data to anyone.
How do I delete my account and data?
+
Email us with the subject "Delete My Account" and we'll permanently remove your account and all associated data within 7 days.
